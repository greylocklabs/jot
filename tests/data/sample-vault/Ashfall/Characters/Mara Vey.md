Mara runs the last ferry across the ash river. She keeps a waxed ledger of every
crossing and never charges children.

## Voice

Short sentences. Answers a question with a question when she is hiding something.

## Relationships

- Older sister of [[Soren]]
- Owes a debt to the harbor master at [[The Ferry]]
